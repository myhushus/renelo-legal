Privacy Policy for Decliner

Version: 1.0
Effective date: October 8, 2026
Developer: Meroviq

Decliner is designed as a local-first Android call management app. It does not require an account and does not use a server-based spam database.

1. Information processed on your device

Decliner may process phone numbers and incoming-call information made available by Android so it can apply the call rules you configure. Decliner may also access contacts if you choose contact-based features. This information is used on your device to evaluate rules such as the Decline List, Allowlist, weekly rules, repeated-call exceptions, and whether a number is in your contacts.

2. Caller ID & spam app role

To manage incoming calls, Decliner must be selected as the Android caller ID & spam app. Android may provide call-related information required for Decliner to decide whether a call should ring normally, be handled with Stealth, or be Blocked.

If another third-party app currently holds this Android role, you may need to choose which app will hold the role. Manufacturer, carrier, or other system-level call protection features may operate separately depending on the device.

3. Contacts

If you grant contacts access, Decliner uses contact information locally to let you select numbers, identify whether a caller is in your contacts, and apply your configured rules. Decliner does not upload your contacts to a Meroviq server and does not use them to build a shared caller or spam database.

4. Filtered-call history

Decliner may keep a local record of incoming calls that Decliner actually handled with Stealth or Block so you can review them in the Filtered section. This is not intended to reproduce your complete system call history.

5. Data transmission and sharing

Decliner does not require a user account and does not operate a backend service for storing your contacts, call rules, or filtered-call history. Decliner does not sell personal information. The app is designed so its call-management data remains on your device unless you intentionally export a backup through Android's system document interface.

6. Backup and restore

If you create a backup, Android lets you choose a destination through the system document interface. Depending on providers available on your device, that destination may be local storage, Google Drive, or another compatible document provider. The storage destination you choose is governed by you and that provider. Meroviq does not receive your backup file as part of Decliner's backup process.

7. Retention and deletion

Decliner stores its app data locally until you delete individual data, reset the app, restore different data, clear app storage, or uninstall the app, as applicable.

8. Permissions and Android roles

Decliner requests permissions and Android roles needed for its features, such as contact access when you use contact-based functions and the caller ID & spam role for incoming-call management. Availability and behavior may vary by Android version and device manufacturer. You can review or change permissions in Android system settings.

9. Children

Decliner is a general-purpose call management utility and is not specifically directed to children.

10. Changes to this policy

Meroviq may update this policy when Decliner's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact information shown on Decliner's Google Play store listing.
